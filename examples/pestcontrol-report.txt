REDEVELOP & RANK PLAYBOOK
================================================================
1. Rebuild in the SAME topical niche (pest control) — Google's expired-domain
   abuse policy (2024+) punishes unrelated-content flips, but a genuine
   pest-control site on a pest-control domain is exactly the legitimate case.
2. Restore old URL structure where sensible: pull the sitemap from Wayback and
   recreate/301 the old URLs (e.g. /services/termites) so legacy backlinks
   resolve instead of 404ing.
3. For a local-business domain (e.g. rightwaypestcontrol.com), targeting the
   SAME metro (St. Louis) preserves the geo-relevance of its old citations
   (directories, chambers, local blogs).
4. Publish real content before pointing anything else at it; let Google recrawl
   for 4-8 weeks. Expect the aged-domain edge to show up as faster indexing and
   easier top-20 entry, not instant #1 — you still need on-page + fresh links.
5. Skip anything with spam score >5, casino/pharma anchors, or CJK-spam history.
